Where your data lives

Web app: everything you create is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to a server and no tracking cookies are used. That also means clearing this site's cookies or site data, or using private browsing, will erase your data — export a backup anytime from Settings → Data & Storage inside the app.

Desktop app: your data is kept in a local file on your computer, so it survives browser cleanups. It also includes the full feature set, like the Automation hub, that the lighter web version leaves out.
